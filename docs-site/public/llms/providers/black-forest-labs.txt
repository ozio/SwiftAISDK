## Notes

Black Forest Labs image and FLUX 3 video generation mirror upstream 2.0.28: `BFL_API_KEY`, `x-key` auth, custom base URL and headers, versioned user-agent suffix, `image`/`imageModel` and `video`/`videoModel` aliases, `black-forest-labs.image` and `black-forest-labs.video` provider IDs, `/v1/{model}` submit URLs, response-supplied polling with trusted `bfl.ai` sibling-host credential forwarding, signed result URLs, provider options and warnings, response/provider metadata, and structured errors. Video supports text-to-video, first/last/timed keyframes, video continuation, draft generation and draft enhancement, settled cost metadata, and per-request polling controls through the existing unary Swift video surface. Language and embedding families remain unsupported.
